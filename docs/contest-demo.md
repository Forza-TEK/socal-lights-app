# City-wide contest demo: run of show

Companion to the tutorial video (`SoCal-Lights-Contest-Tutorial.mp4`, 5 min 38 s, captioned, no narration)

Chapters in the video: 0:10 Create the contest · 1:46 A neighbor enters their home · 2:26 Review entries and start voting · 3:08 Judges score the homes · 3:43 The public votes · 4:09 Review votes and announce winners · 4:56 Winners, archives and badges. Steps below match the video chapters.

## Before the demo (15 minutes, the day before)
1. **Sign-in works:** open `https://socallights.app/profile` and sign in with Google and with an emailed code. If either fails, fix it first (`docs/contests.md`, "Turning it on").
2. **Email works:** request a code to a second address and confirm it arrives from `login@socallights.app`. Winner and judge emails use the same sender.
3. **Make a practice contest** with an email at your organization's website so it goes live immediately (a Gmail address waits for approval). Delete or leave it; it is closed by deleting the account or letting its dates pass.
4. **Two phones and a laptop:** laptop = organizer dashboard; phone A = neighbor entering and voting; phone B = a judge. Use two different email addresses.
5. **Pre-load a few entries** so the dashboard and results are not empty. Entries need photos taken or chosen on the phone.
6. **Turn on the light or dark mode** you prefer (circle at the top right of every page).

## The demo, step by step (about 12 minutes live)
1. **Create the contest (3 min).** `socallights.app/add`, choose "A home decorating contest." Name it, pick the city, dates, categories ("Best Overall" is just a category), who votes (everyone / judges / both), invite judges, prizes, and read the official rules. Sign in at the end. Show the QR poster.
2. **Neighbor enters (2 min).** Scan the QR code on the phone. Sign in, add photos, address (use the suggestions), name, accept the rules.
3. **Review and start voting (2 min).** Dashboard: approve entries, "Start now" for voting, copy link, show the judges card.
4. **Judges (1 min).** Phone B opens the invitation link, scores homes 1 to 10 (or picks a favorite), and shows "I know this household."
5. **Public voting (1 min).** One vote per person per category; counts are hidden until the winners are announced.
6. **Integrity and announce (2 min).** Review votes: flagged patterns, void a vote, "Disqualify at close" on an entry. Announce winners; point out the warning when a judge has not finished, the locked results, and the emailed results.
7. **After (1 min).** Public contest page with winners and prizes, Contest archives (every contestant and result, by Season | Year), and the winner's Badges tab in their profile.

## Points worth saying out loud
- Entering and voting are free for the public; votes are never sold.
- One vote per person per category, tied to a verified email; throwaway email addresses are blocked; nobody can vote for their own home.
- Vote counts are hidden while voting so people don't just vote for the leader.
- Judges vote on their own ballot (never in the public vote) and can recuse from homes they know. With 5+ judges the highest and lowest score are dropped.
- Results are locked when announced and kept in the archive exactly as they ended.
- Official rules are drafted for the organizer; if there is a prize, have a lawyer read them.

## Likely questions
- **Can residents enter without an account?** No: a quick sign-in is required so each entry and vote belongs to one verified person.
- **Can we run it for several categories or neighborhoods?** Yes: add your own categories (for example "Best Inflatable") or run one contest per neighborhood.
- **Can businesses sponsor prizes?** Yes: add one prize for everyone or a different prize per award, with a "provided by" line.
- **Where do winners show up?** On the contest page, in Contest archives, and as a crown badge on their listings on the map.
