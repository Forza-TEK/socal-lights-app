## What to show that fits Covina
- **Judging:** judges are invited by email, score or pick homes on their own ballot, and the **"Visit the homes" routes** (nine stops at a time, with tick-offs) fit evening drives. Judges can recuse from homes they know.
- **Residents only:** choose "I review each entry first" so staff approve each address.
- **Entries online:** one link and QR code for flyers, with the poster and yard-sign printables (their prize is a yard sign; ours are for the contest, not the prize).
- **Results:** locked when announced, entrants emailed, a permanent page under `/past`, and a crown badge for winners on the map.
- **Your existing map presence:** point to their Halloween winners and tree lighting on `socallights.app/in/covina`.

## Questions to ask Covina specifically
- Is it the same format as 2025 (judges only), or do you want a public People's Choice too?
- Entry dates, judging nights, winner date? Who are the judges and how many?
- Do you keep the yard-sign and social-media prize? Any other prize or sponsor to name (the law wants an approximate value stated)?
- How do residents prove they live in Covina, and do you want entries approved by staff?
- Do you use your own rules text and release forms? Can you paste them?
- Who else should have access (parks, library, communications)?
