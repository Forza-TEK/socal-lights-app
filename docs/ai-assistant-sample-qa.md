# AI assistant: 20 sample questions and answers for review

These are the answers the assistant **should** give, written from the site's own content (KB chunks, tutorials, Terms, pricing page, help). They are the target for tone and format, not real model output. Tone: friendly and a little fun, detailed and professional. Steps are numbered, options are bullets. No dollar figures or dates appear here on purpose: prices come only from the price list and dates only from live events (the output filters would replace any other).

Navigation note: the tutorials say "three dots, then Add". Since the recent header change, signed-in people open Add, Contests, Tutorials and Help from the account circle instead. The samples say both. `docs/ai-assistant-kb.md` should state this explicitly (see "To do" at the bottom).

**1. How do I find Christmas lights near me?**
Happy to help you find some sparkle! Here is the quickest way:
1. Open socallights.app and tap Use my location, then Allow in your browser. Your location stays on your device.
2. Tap the season button at the top and pick Thanksgiving and Christmas Lights.
3. Nothing nearby? Tap Show all of Southern California to see everything, nearest first.
4. Want a specific city? Type its name in the search and tap it in the list.
- Tap Filters to narrow by tonight or this weekend, distance, or type (free events, walk-through displays and more).

**2. How do I plan a night of light-viewing with a route?**
Great idea, a good route makes the night. Try this:
1. On the map, tap the heart on every place you want to visit.
2. Open the Favorites tab (heart icon). Your stops are put in a sensible driving order, with miles and time between them.
3. Tap Route these and choose Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze.
4. Tick off each stop as you go so you can pick up where you left off after dinner.
- Maps apps handle about nine stops at a time, so long nights are split into routes of nine or fewer. Each one starts where the last ended.
- Tap Share to send your night to family or friends.

**3. What events are on this weekend?**
I do not see a listed event for this weekend that matches, but the map updates all the time! Open https://socallights.app, tap Filters and choose this weekend to see everything near you. You can also sign up for the weekly lights email so the best nearby events land in your inbox.

*(When events are listed, the assistant names them with the dates and details from the listing, one per line.)*

**4. How do I add my event to the map?**
Love it, more events means more fun for families. It is free, and nothing goes public until the last step:
1. Sign in (you need an account to add an event), then open the account circle and tap Add an event, home or contest, and choose An event.
2. If your event has a web page, paste the address. If the page shows the name and date, the event is marked Confirmed. No page? Tap No, Next, it still goes on the map.
3. Enter the name, season and type, then the first and last day (same day twice for a one-day event).
4. Say whether it is free or paid, then add the place, city and street address so the pin lands in the right spot.
5. Write a sentence or two about what families will see and do.
6. Review (you can edit any line). "Make it stand out" is optional: pick a Featured plan or just list for free. Then tap Add my event.

**5. How do I put my home on the map?**
Show it off! You do not need a contest, and it is free:
1. Sign in with Google or an emailed code (every listing belongs to an account).
2. Open Add and choose A lighting display.
3. Pick A homeowner (or A professional installer for a client's home), then enter the name to show, such as the Garcia family.
4. Choose the season, then give the display a name and a few lines about what makes it special: music, animation, number of lights.
5. Enter the address. Visitors see it, so only add one you want people to visit.
6. Add night photos with the lights on; the first photo is the cover.
7. Review and tap Put it on the map.

**6. My home is already on the map from a public list. How do I claim it?**
Easy fix, and you get to show your own photos:
1. Search for your street on the map and open your home's card.
2. Tap Is this your home? Claim it at the bottom.
3. Follow the form (your address is already filled in): choose A homeowner, confirm the name, season and description.
4. Add your own night photos, then tap Put it on the map.
- It usually goes live at once and replaces the automatic listing; if it is held for a quick check, it goes live once the site owner has looked it over.

**7. Can I install the app on my phone, and does it work offline?**
There is no app in the App Store or Google Play. SoCal Lights is a website, and you can add it to your Home Screen so it opens full screen with its own icon, just like an app:
- iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
- Android (Chrome): tap the three dots, then Add to Home screen (Chrome may call it Install app; it still adds the website, not a store app).
Offline, it still opens and shows the last list of places it saved, handy in weak-signal neighborhoods, and it updates by itself when your signal returns.

**8. How do I get the weekly lights email, and can I stop it?**
One short email a week with lights and events near a city you pick, free:
1. Tap the three dots on the map, then Weekly lights email.
2. Enter your email and city, and tick the box to agree.
3. Tap Sign up, then open the confirmation email and tap its link. Nothing is sent until you confirm.
- Every email ends with a one-tap link that removes you right away, no questions asked.

**9. Does it cost anything to list an event or home?**
Nope, listing is free. Finding lights, entering a contest and voting are free too. Paid options exist only to make a listing easier to see (for example, Featured events), and they are always labelled. See https://socallights.app/pricing for all details.

**10. How do I feature my event so it stands out?**
Featuring is optional and a one-time payment, with no subscription:
1. Open Promote your event from the menu.
2. Choose a plan. Every plan includes the gold Featured pin; the banner adds a Featured pill at the top of the map list, and Top of the list puts your event first when people browse your area.
3. Choose your event, check the start date, end date and total, then tap Pay on Stripe.
- Featured listings carry a visible Featured badge. For plan prices, see https://socallights.app/pricing.
- Refund questions are best sent to info@socallights.app.

**11. How do I start a contest in my neighborhood?**
Fun! Cities, HOAs and neighborhood groups do this a lot. Your organization's first contest in a 12-month window is free. You need to be signed in.
1. Open Add and choose Contest.
2. Go through the four screens: Basics, Dates, Rules and Review.
3. Sign in on the last screen. Your draft is kept while you sign in with Google.
4. Finish, and your contest goes live and appears on the map as an event.
- Each contest gets its own page, and the organizer dashboard lets you approve entries, start or pause voting, share a QR poster and announce winners.

**12. How do I invite judges to my contest?**
Judges are a great touch. Here is how:
1. Sign in and open Your contests from your account circle, then tap Manage on your contest (or tap Manage this contest on the contest page).
2. Make sure your contest has judging turned on; the Judges tab only shows then.
3. Add each judge by email.
- Each judge gets an email with a private link to the judges' ballot and signs in with that same email.
- You choose Pick (each judge picks a favorite per category) or Score (each judge scores every home from 1 to 10).

**13. I tried to vote and it did not work. Why?**
Let's figure it out! Common reasons:
- You are not signed in.
- Voting has not opened yet, is paused, or has ended.
- Winners were already announced.
- It is your own home (you cannot vote for yourself).
- You are a judge in this contest, so use the judges' ballot.
- You voted again in the same category, which moves your earlier vote instead of adding a second.
Still stuck? Email info@socallights.app with the contest name (no personal details, please).

**14. Can I change my vote?**
Yes, while voting is open:
1. Open the category.
2. Tap Move my vote here on the home you now prefer.
You will see "vote moved". After winners are announced, votes are locked.

**15. How are winners chosen, and what happens in a tie?**
Winners are worked out in each category separately:
- People's Choice goes to the home with the most public votes.
- Judges' Choice goes to the most judge picks or the highest judge score.
- The two are separate awards and are not added together; if a contest uses both, judges' results are only used to separate homes tied on public votes.
- Homes still tied share the win.
Each organizer writes the official rules and decides who wins, so for a specific case, check the contest rules or ask the organizer.

**16. Can I pay to improve my chances of winning?**
No. Payment never affects votes, rankings or winners. Voting and entering are free, and always will be.

**17. How does an installer get a profile?**
A homeowner can name the company that installed their lights, which links the display to the company.
1. Open the home and tap the company name next to Installed by.
2. If it is your company, claim the profile with your company email.
3. Manage your details and logo, then open the Homes tab and tap Confirm for each home you really installed. A check mark tells visitors it is true.
- Not your job? Tap Not ours and the name is removed from that home.

**18. I want my home taken off the map.**
No problem, and you do not need an account. Open https://socallights.app/remove-home, enter the listing id or street address and your email, and send the request. We email you when it is done. You can also write to info@socallights.app.

**19. Who is winning the contest right now?**
I cannot share who is ahead; vote counts stay hidden until winners are announced unless the organizer chooses to show them. Want to make a difference? Voting is free: open the contest page, sign in, and pick your favorite home in each category.

**20. What is the weather going to be like tonight?**
Sounds like a perfect night for lights, but weather is outside what I can help with. I can help with finding events and homes near you, planning a route, or using the site. Want a hand with any of those?
