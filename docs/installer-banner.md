# Installer banner

A thin strip labelled "Sponsor" on the map: company name (links to the website, or to a mailto link if there is no website) | phone (tap to call).
Always at the top of the list panel (phone portrait, landscape, tablet and desktop), above the Places tabs. It is hidden when the list is minimized.
Height 24px (the smallest comfortable tap target; WCAG 2.2 minimum). The first brief was one third of the search field (17px); the owner chose 24px.

## Who shows
`installers.banner_until` (YYYY-MM-DD). Set in Admin > Installers > Profile > "Map banner". Empty = not on the banner. The profile must be active and have a phone or website.
Sold, not a free pilot (the owner drives visitors with Facebook and Instagram ads). Installers now buy it themselves at `/sponsor` (see "Payments" below). An admin can still set the dates by hand.

## Which installers show for the current map view
`/api/banner` returns every installer on the banner (same for everyone, cached). `public/banner.js` picks the ones to show:
- The map view's cities (by centre) and counties (from the places in view, plus the nearest city to the centre) are matched to the installer's "Areas served" list ("Orange County, Irvine").
- An installer with no areas listed, or "Southern California"/"All", shows everywhere.
- It is a scrolling marquee: each matching installer is its own pill, followed by one dashed "Installers: advertise here" pill (links to /pricing#banner), which is also all that shows when nobody matches. The pause button sits next to the Sponsor label (right end on a portrait phone).
- It pauses on hover and focus and has a pause button. With reduced motion on, it starts paused and the play button starts it.
- The starting installer changes each visit so nobody is always first. A "shown" is counted when the pill is actually on screen.

## Numbers
`banner_stats` (daily): times shown (once per installer per page visit), company-link taps, phone taps. Totals only, nothing personal. Shown to admins (installer profile, last 30 days) and to the installer in their dashboard.

## Not built yet
A per-county cap, an "Advertise here" placeholder when the banner is empty, a monthly report email.

## Prices on the pricing page (owner, 2026-11-06)
- Free: profile card (page at /installer/<slug>, linked from the homes they installed).
- $50 per season: the Sponsor banner. Set by an admin with the "Show on the banner until" date.
- $75 per season: banner plus the company card at the top of the list behind Featured events. The list card is built (migration 0170): `installers.top_until`, set in Admin > Installers > Profile > "Top of the list until" (set the banner date too: the $75 tier includes the banner). It shows up to two labelled "Sponsor" cards at the top of the list, right behind the Featured places, for installers whose areas are in the map view (same matching as the banner). Numbers (shown, taps) are in `banner_stats` (`lst_*`) and appear in Admin and the installer's dashboard.
- Sold at `/sponsor` (below). Email info@socallights.app is only for questions.

## Payments (built 2026-11-06)
Funnel: `/pricing#banner` has a **Choose** button on each paid tier (`/sponsor?plan=banner|banner_top`; the free tier's button goes to `/add-house?kind=installer`). `/sponsor` steps: sign in (the plan choice survives the sign-in), choose the installer profile, choose the plan, check the exact dates and price, Stripe Checkout. Back from Stripe: `/sponsor?paid=1` or `?cancelled=1`. The installer dashboard (`/installer/<slug>/manage`) has a Sponsor placement card with status, dates, numbers and Renew or upgrade.
- **Prices:** `INSTALLER_PLANS` in `functions/_lib/featured.js` (the only place; `/api/config` serves them as `installerPlans`). No page holds a price (a test checks `public/`).
- **Who can buy:** the signed-in owner of the profile (`installers.owner_email`, same rule as the dashboard, helper `ownsInstaller`). No profile: the page offers "Create your free profile first" and a search with Claim this profile. The profile must be active with a phone or a website (blocking, with a link to fix it); no areas listed is advice only.
- **Season rule** (`functions/_lib/sponsor.js`, tested in `tests/sponsor-rules.test.mjs`): a placement runs from the day it starts to the last day of the sponsor season that day falls in. Sponsor seasons (owner decision 2026-11-06) follow the map windows in `public/seasons.js` (which are not changed) except around the new year: **Christmas lights season Nov 3 to Jan 6** (the map's own Christmas window ends Dec 26 and it shows New Year's from Dec 27, but a purchase on any date from Nov 3 through Jan 6 ends Jan 6), **New Year's & Lunar New Year Jan 7 to Jan 31**, then Valentine's Feb 1-15, Spring Feb 16-May 15, Summer May 16-Aug 31, Halloween Sep 1-Nov 2. The table is `sponsorSeasonTable()` in `functions/_lib/sponsor.js`; the end year of the Christmas season is computed from its start date. A placement ending Jan 6 renews from Jan 7 into the next season.
- **Price (owner decision 2026-11-06, final): no proration.** A plan costs its full price (Banner $50, Banner and top of the list $75, `INSTALLER_PLANS` in `functions/_lib/featured.js`) whenever it is bought during a season, and the placement runs until the season's last day. It is the installer's decision to weigh cost against timing; there is no refund or credit for timing. The pages state the exact start date, end date and number of days (counting the first day) and the price before payment, for example "Runs from today until Jan 6, the end of the Thanksgiving & Christmas Lights season (62 days, counting the first day). The price is the same whenever you start." (This replaces the earlier proration, option C and the per-plan floors; none of that is in the code any more.)
- **Renewal:** buying the same plan while it runs starts the day after it ends (the first day of the next season, full price) and runs to that season's end. Time is only added, never shortened. At most one season ahead is sold (the horizon is the end of the season after the one a purchase today reaches); beyond that checkout answers 400 "already booked through ...".
- **Upgrade:** Banner to Banner and top of the list, when the banner already runs to the end of the season the top card would reach, costs the **difference between the two plans' prices** (`INSTALLER_PLANS`: $25 today; not hardcoded). The top card starts today and runs to the season end; the banner stays as it is. If the banner would end sooner, it is the full price of the new plan, and the banner is extended to match. No refund or credit for timing. The page says this plainly.
- **Webhook** (`functions/api/stripe-webhook.js`, `applyInstallerOrder` / `revokeInstaller` in `sponsor.js`): orders are `feature_orders` rows with `target='installer'`, `event_id` = installer id, plan `banner` or `banner_top` (migration 0171 adds `applied_top_until`, `applied_from`, `prev_banner_until`, `prev_top_until`). Same dedupe, retry (an unknown profile answers 500 so Stripe retries), refund, partial refund (held), dispute, dispute won and refund-first handling as Featured. A refund or lost dispute switches the placement off, putting back what an admin had set by hand and keeping time other paid orders still cover; a longer date an admin set by hand afterwards is left on and the order gets a "placement differs" note.
- **Admin:** Orders shows the installer's name, plan, amount and dates. Admin > Installers refuses a banner or top date earlier than a paid order that is still running (refund the order first). Merging profiles moves the paid orders and keeps the later dates.
- **Emails:** Stripe's own receipt (receipt_email), a service email to the buyer (`sponsor_paid`, no unsubscribe or postal line: it is transactional) and a note to the team (`sponsor_admin`). Both are editable in Admin > Email templates.
- **Counters** (`site_hits`, no personal data; shown in Admin > Dashboard "How people use the site" and the digest): `fn:pricing-choose`, `fn:sponsor-shown`, `fn:sponsor-picked` (browser), `fn:sponsor-checkout`, `fn:sponsor-paid` (server).
- **Rules that stay:** paid placements are always labelled "Sponsor"; payment never touches a contest (a test checks); the map stays free; web only (hidden in the app, 403 for the app's user agent).

## Optional automatic renewal (built 2026-11-07; installers only, default OFF)
- **Opt in:** an UNTICKED box on `/sponsor` before paying ("Renew automatically each season: we charge your saved card on the last day of your placement at the plan price shown then (currently $X)... Cancel any time..."), or "Turn on automatic renewal" in the installer dashboard (a Stripe setup page saves the card; nothing is charged). The wording is a versioned constant (`CONSENT_TEXTS` in `functions/_lib/renewal.js`, served by `/api/config` as `installerRenewal`); the version and time are stored with the consent. Stripe keeps the card (`setup_future_usage=off_session`); we store only the Stripe customer and payment-method ids in `installer_autorenew` (migration 0176). Never a card number.
- **Emails** (all editable in Admin > Email templates): enrolled (acknowledgment with the wording and the cancel link), reminder (7 days before: amount, date, plan, next dates, a price-change note, cancel link), charged (receipt), failed (link to pay or update the card), lapsed, cancelled; the team gets `renewal_admin` when a renewal lapses or is paused.
- **Daily job:** `.github/workflows/sponsor-renewals.yml` (15:17 UTC) calls `POST /api/sponsor/renewals` with the existing `ALERT_CRON_KEY` secret (no new secret). It sends reminders (once per renewal), and on the charge date (the last day of the running placement, Pacific) creates ONE off-session PaymentIntent at the plan's CURRENT price (`INSTALLER_PLANS`), idempotency key per installer + season, with metadata `target=installer`, `plan`, `renewal=1`. The paid order goes through the same code as a Checkout payment (`applyPaid` in `stripe-webhook.js`): the order is keyed by the PaymentIntent id, so the job and the `payment_intent.succeeded` webhook can both deliver it and it is recorded once; the amount is checked against the server calculation; the next placement starts the day after the running one ends and ends on the next season's last day (`sponsorSeasonTable()`); at most one season ahead (if the owner already renewed by hand, the date moves and nothing is charged).
- **Failure:** a decline or requires-action answer emails a link to pay once or update the card, retries ONCE 3 days later (its own idempotency key), then sets status `lapsed` and emails once; no more automatic charges after a lapse.
- **Cancel:** the link in any email opens `/cancel-renewal?t=<token>` (unguessable 36-hex token, no login; opening it changes nothing, the button cancels) or "Turn off" in the dashboard. It keeps the placement already paid for and asks Stripe to detach the saved card. Cancelling before the charge prevents it.
- **Refund or dispute** of an automatic charge (or of the order that enrolled it) switches the placement off like any order and pauses the renewal (status `paused`) and emails the team.
- **Admin:** Orders shows a read-only "Automatic renewals" list (upcoming, failed, lapsed, paused) and tags charges "Auto-renewal"; the daily digest has a one-line summary. Counters: `fn:autorenew-opt-in`, `-charged`, `-failed`, `-cancelled`.
- **Account deletion** deletes the renewal row and detaches the saved payment method (best effort). **Terms, Privacy, Pricing and /sponsor** describe it; `tests/sponsor-renewal.test.mjs` checks the Terms wording against the code constants.

### Audit 2026-11-07: what was fixed and what is consciously deferred
Fixed in the same release: **H2** (one real Pacific `ptToday()` with daylight saving in `featured.js`; `banner/index.js`, `banner/hit.js` and the dashboard use it, so a banner shows through its last Pacific day; tests incl. 4:30 pm on the last day and the PDT midnight), **M1** (checkout stores `quote_from`, `quote_to`, `quote_cents` in the session and on the order; the webhook applies what was quoted, and an order whose end date or price no longer fits, for example a stale second tab or a $75 payment after the banner was bought, is NOT applied: it becomes `refund_due` with a note), **M2** (an upgrade is decided by the period: banner + top costs the $25 difference whenever the banner already covers the period, running top card or not), **PL-02** (installer copy no longer says "no subscription / no automatic renewal"; Featured events and homes still never renew; a test checks both), and the ARL checklist in `docs/audit/2026-11-07/privacy-legal.md` section 3 (consent text, version, time, user and plan stored; acknowledgment email; online cancel without login; reminder; price-change note; deletion handling; disclosure beside an unticked box).
From the 24-item failure list in the payments audit, handled: 4 (card only, customer created), 5 (ids only, deleted with the account), 6 and 7 (an attempt row is claimed before Stripe is called; an unknown outcome repeats the same idempotency key), 9 and 10 (`payment_intent.succeeded`, order keyed by the PaymentIntent id, Checkout PaymentIntents ignored by metadata), 11 (recomputed with `sponsorWindow` at charge time, one-season-ahead horizon re-checked), 12 (Pacific dates), 13 (decline and requires-action: email with a pay link, one retry, lapse), 14 (the agreeing user must still own the profile, hidden profile lapses it), 15 (refund or dispute pauses), 16 (cancel is honored up to the claim), 17 (a Stripe outage is not a decline: the attempt stays pending, logged in ops_log), 18 (the job is idempotent and catches up a missed day; the digest shows when it last ran and flags overdue charges), 19 (hidden in the app), 20 (Admin list and tag), 2 (fail closed: no reminder on record means no charge).
Deferred on purpose: 8 (a charge that succeeds but fails to apply is recorded by the `payment_intent.succeeded` redelivery and by the next run; there is no separate reconcile job or "pending" order row), 21 (Stripe test clocks; the in-memory tests cover the paths), 22 (sent emails are not logged; `notice_sent_for` records the reminder), 23 (Stripe Billing instead of a home-made job; season end dates vary), 24 (tax: CPA), and the point that auto-renewing the Christmas season into New Year's (Jan 7 to Jan 31, 25 days) charges the full price for a short, low-value season: the reminder shows the next dates and the price, and the owner can change this rule.
