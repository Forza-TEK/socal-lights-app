# Audit 2026-11-11: pricing, payments, emails and trust

Read-only audit of the money and trust surfaces of SoCalLights.app. Branch `audit-money`. No product code was changed.

**How it was tested.**
- *Live site* (https://socallights.app), anonymous GET only: `/pricing` (all four tabs), `/feature`, `/sponsor`, `/partner`, `/contest-pass`, `/cancel-renewal`, `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/api/config`, Help articles, tutorial caption files. Playwright at 440x956, 320x568, 820x1180, 1280x900 and 220 px wide (200% text zoom of an iPhone), keyboard Tab pass, and the in-app user agent `SoCalLightsApp/1.0`. Nothing was signed in, submitted or paid on the live site.
- *Local copy* (`wrangler pages dev`, local D1, dev sign-in, a deliberately fake Stripe key so no payment page could open): signed-in states of `/contest-pass`, `/partner`, `/sponsor`, `/feature`, the `?paid=1` and `?cancelled=1` return pages, and the "payment page could not be opened" error path.
- *Emails*: every template in `functions/_lib/mailtpl.js` were rendered to HTML and plain text with their sample values, and the money ones were screenshotted at 600 and 375 px, light and dark.
- *Reading code*: `functions/_lib/{passes,partner,featured,sponsor,stripe,passmail,partnermail,featuremail,sponsormail,mailtpl}.js`, the four `checkout.js` endpoints, `stripe-webhook.js`.

Findings are tagged **[T]** confirmed by testing or **[C]** from reading code only (not exercised).

## Summary (top 10)

1. **PAY-01 (P1) [T]** On `/contest-pass`, every status message (payment cancelled, payment page failed to open, "confirming your pass", "your pass is active") is drawn below the whole pass picker, about 1,500 px below the button that was tapped. On a failed Choose the buyer sees the button flip back and nothing else.
2. **PAY-02 (P1) [C]** Buying a Featured pin or Top of the list for a *home* sends no confirmation email, although the Pricing FAQ says "Stripe emails the receipt, and we send a confirmation."
3. **PAY-03 (P1) [C]** When a payment is taken but not applied (held as `refund_due`: quote changed, duplicate, event declined or removed), or when a refund or dispute switches a placement off, the buyer gets no email from us at all. Only Stripe's receipt.
4. **PAY-04 (P1) [T]** Privacy says "event hosts can pay to feature an event" and keeps order records "for Featured placements". It never mentions contest passes or Event Partner (no `pass` or `Event Partner` text anywhere in the policy).
5. **PAY-05 (P2) [T]** Refund wording is inconsistent: receipts say "Questions or a refund: write to...", the Pricing FAQ (shown on every tab, event wording only) says non-refundable once live, and no page says what happens to an unused contest pass or Event Partner plan.
6. **PAY-06 (P2) [T]** In the app, Pricing hides the paid cards but still shows the refund, "add a part later" and "season" FAQs; the Homeowners text ends in a colon with nothing after it; the Contest tab talks about a pass with no pass or explanation.
7. **PAY-07 (P2) [T+C]** Pricing FAQ says "card or Cash App Pay"; Terms say "by card"; the code never sets payment methods (left to the Stripe dashboard).
8. **PAY-08 (P2) [C]** No tax handling (no `automatic_tax`) and no word about sales tax anywhere. Flag for the owner's accountant.
9. **PAY-10 (P2) [T]** Emails are forced light (`color-scheme: light`, no dark styles); the copyright line is 3.0:1 and the white-on-orange button 3.8:1.
10. **PAY-12 (P2) [C]** Stale prices remain in code comments and docs (`docs/monetization.md`, `docs/stripe-setup.md`, `passes.js:3`, `contest-pass/checkout.js:1`, `banner.js:137`).

**Price consistency result (good news).** Every user-facing surface agrees with the current truth: `/api/config`, `/pricing` (four tabs), `/feature`, `/partner`, `/contest-pass`, `/sponsor`, Terms, the Help articles (`contest-passes`, `event-partner-plan`, `feature-an-event-or-home`, `sponsor-your-installer-company`), the tutorial caption files (`run-passes*.vtt`), the chat assistant knowledge (`chatkb-data.js`, which deliberately holds no dollar amounts) and `docs/marketing/ads-content.json`. No old `$50/$75/$100` pass, "first contest is free", or `$10/$15/$25` event plan was found in any of them. Live `/api/config`: pass_1 14900, pass_5 29900, pass_20 49900, over 20 contact us, `free: 0`; Event Partner 14900 / cap 10; event components 2500 each (tiers 25/50/50/75); homes pin 1000 / top 1500; installer banner 5000, top 5000, both 7500.

## What works well

- Prices come from one place per product (`passes.js`, `partner.js`, `featured.js`) and are served by `/api/config`; pages only hold wording. Live values match the stated truth everywhere checked.
- Checkout is server-priced: amounts, dates and organization come from the server, not the browser; double-click protection (idempotency), a per-account cap on payment pages, quote locked in metadata and checked by the webhook.
- Payment never touches contests: the "money never changes a contest result" statement appears on Pricing, every paid page, Terms, Help and the emails.
- In-app: all paid checkouts return 403 and the paid cards are hidden; `/contest-pass` and `/partner` explain "bought on the website".
- Return pages: `/partner` and `/feature?cancelled=1` cancel states are clear and keep choices; `/contest-pass?pass=paid` polls until the pass is active and says "you will not be charged again" if slow.
- Renewal emails are thorough (reminder 7 days ahead, cancel link without login, failed-charge retry explained, lapse notice); `/cancel-renewal` without a token fails gracefully with a human fallback.
- Emails render without overflow at 375 and 600 px; plain-text parts are present, links are spelled out, subjects are specific and carry the name or amount.
- Keyboard: Tab order on Pricing is logical, focus outlines visible (2 px), choose links carry full aria-labels ("Choose Single contest, $149").
- Help, tutorial captions and the chat knowledge base are free of stale prices.
