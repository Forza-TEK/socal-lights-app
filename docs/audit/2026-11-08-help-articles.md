# Help article audit, 2026-11-08 (second pass)

Scope: every Help Center article against the product as it is now. The first pass (migration `0199_help_audit_2026-11-08.sql`, PR 458) rewrote 43 of the 54 articles on 2026-11-07; `0200_help_route_title.sql` fixed one title. This pass re-checked all 54 against `origin/main` at `333772a`, found gaps, and fixes them with one add-only migration, `0201_help_audit_gaps_2026-11-08.sql` (generated by `scripts/help/updates-audit-1108b.mjs`).

Result: 54 existing articles (8 updated, 46 already correct) and 5 new articles, so 59 once `0201` applies.

## How it was checked
- **Live set.** Applied every migration to a scratch SQLite database (`tests/_d1.mjs`) and read `help_articles`: 54 articles before `0201`, 59 after. Help text also exists in `functions/_lib/help.js` (topic names and blurbs) and `functions/help/index.js` (page text); both were read.
- **Owner edits.** Fetched every article page from https://socallights.app/help/<slug> and compared the title and body with the migration text. All 54 match exactly, so no article has been changed in Admin (a changed body or title would differ). Not detectable from outside: an Admin edit that changed only the one-sentence answer, the search words, the order or the draft status. The migration's `updated_by = 'seed'` guard skips any such article anyway. Owner check: `SELECT slug, updated_by FROM help_articles WHERE updated_by <> 'seed';` in the D1 console.
- **Labels.** Every quoted button, tab, menu and page name was searched for in `public/*.html`, `public/*.js` and `functions/` (about 100 strings; the only misses were "Pay on Stripe", which the page builds as "Pay <price> on Stripe", and "Open in Google Maps", built as "Open in " plus the app name). On the live site (Playwright, desktop Chrome) I confirmed: the map's locate button is labeled "Find my location"; the three dots menu lists Add, Weekly lights email, Support SoCal Lights, Promote your event, Contests, Browse by city, Tutorials, Help, Privacy & terms, Contact us; the Add page tiles are "An event", "A contest", "A lighting display"; "Route these" is on the map; the "Ask SoCal Lights" button shows on the map and on Pricing, and `/api/config` says `chatEnabled: true`. The local app could not be started (the Workers AI binding needs a Cloudflare login), so contest dashboard screens were checked in source, not on screen; the 2026-11-08 video re-records (PRs 461 to 471) already exercised them.
- **Numbers and rules against code.** No article contains a dollar figure (searched `\$[0-9]`), so none can drift from `functions/_lib/featured.js` and `passes.js`. Pass names (Up to 5, Up to 10, Up to 20 contests; one free contest; 12-month window; more than 20 means contact us) match `passes.js`. Season end "Christmas lights: Jan 6", Christmas season Nov 3 to Jan 6 and the next season Jan 7 to Jan 31 match `functions/_lib/sponsor.js`. Featuring opens 30 days before an event and home plans last 30 days (`featured.js`). Limits quoted in articles match code: 8 photos, 5 entries per person per contest (`functions/api/submit.js`), 8 co-organizers (`staff.js`), 500 homes added per contest, 25 while a new contest awaits review (`UNTRUSTED_ADDED_CAP`), 1,000 homes an hour, organizer sponsor logos 600 KB and receipts 5 MB, route length of 9 stops. "Everything in" appears in no article. "House" and "display" appear only in slugs, quoted screen names (A lighting display, the Displays tab) and one article title, now fixed.

## Open decisions
1. **Add-homes limit shown on screen.** The Add homes yourself box says "Up to 100 at a time" (`public/organizer.html` line 141), but its Check addresses button accepts up to 500 lines per paste (line 320; 500 is also the per-contest cap). The article repeats "up to 100 at a time", which is safe advice. Pick one number and make the hint, the check and the article agree.
2. **Add page says a contest is "Free · about 3 minutes".** Only the first contest in 12 months is free; later ones need a pass. The Add tile in `public/add.html` does not say so. Suggest "First one free".
3. **Monthly update is not being sent yet** (`docs/status.md`: "The newsletter is not built or sent"). The article describes only the opt-in and the controls, with no schedule. Review the wording before the first send.
4. **UI words still saying house or display.** Add tile "A lighting display", map type chip "Home displays", three dots row "a home display", profile tab "Displays", Admin "Houses waiting" and "Published houses". Articles quote the ones people see. If the owner renames them, update the articles in the same change.
5. **Videos (not touched here, for the video audit):** `add-a-home-without-a-contest` could use the existing `post-house` video; `sponsor-a-contest-with-an-invite-link` could use `sponsors-invite`, which already shows the sponsor's `/s/<token>` page. `contest-passes` stays without a video (2026-11-07 decision); the assistant, monthly update and stop-emails articles need none. Setting `video` on an article is an Admin edit or a later migration.
6. **Admin edits not visible from outside:** run the SQL under "Owner edits" once if you want certainty.
