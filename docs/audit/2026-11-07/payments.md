# Payments and finance audit (2026-11-07)

Scope: read-only audit of every money path on `origin/main` at 668b381 (PR 379). Method: read code and docs, ran `npm test` (177 pass, 0 fail), ran throwaway experiments against the in-memory D1 (scratch files outside the repo), GET-only reads of the live site (`/api/config`, `/api/banner`, `/pricing`, `/sponsor`, `/feature`, `/terms`, three Help articles). No Stripe call, no payment, no email, no write to production. The live `orders` table and Stripe were not readable from here, so "what production holds" comes from docs (0 orders, 0 revenue as of 2026-11-04) and from the live pages. Application code was not changed.

## Top 5 (read these first)

1. **H1. The owner cannot tell that a customer paid in Stripe but we never recorded it.** A webhook with a bad signature, a rotated secret, or a Cloudflare WAF challenge returns 400 and logs nothing (`functions/api/stripe-webhook.js:20`); Admin > Orders is explicitly "read-only: nothing here calls Stripe" (`functions/api/admin/orders.js:2`); there is no Stripe-versus-D1 report. Also, no real signed webhook has ever reached production (the $10 test purchase is still unchecked in `docs/CHECKLIST.md:46`), and the zone's bot protection is known to challenge data-center IPs (`.github/workflows/submission-digest.yml:5`). Sponsor checkout is live today (`/api/config` shows `featureCheckout: true`, `/sponsor` returns 200).
2. **H2. Sponsor banners stop 8 hours early, and a last-day purchase shows nothing at all.** The banner endpoints compare `banner_until` with the UTC date while the sale, the quote and the webhook use Pacific time. Reproduced: a banner paid "through Nov 2" is served at 12:00 Pacific on Nov 2 and gone at 16:30 Pacific the same day. A buyer who pays $50 after 4 pm Pacific on a season's last day gets a placement that is never shown.
3. **H3. Auto-renewal must not ship before the copy and consent change.** Five places promise the opposite today: `public/terms.html:53`, `:64`, `public/pricing.html:45`, `public/sponsor.html:35`, plus the "no subscription" lines in `public/feature.html:135,169`. There is no design doc on `main`; the failure-mode list for the builder is in section 5. The current webhook is Checkout-only and the order table is keyed by Checkout session id, so off-session charges need new handlers, a new key and new Stripe event subscriptions.
4. **M1. The sponsor window is re-calculated when the webhook arrives, not locked at checkout.** Two payment pages for the same plan (opened more than 10 minutes apart) both pay at $50 and the second silently books the next season with no flag (experiment E3). A banner+top payment of $75 made while a banner is already running is only flagged as a mismatch and still applied (customer overpays $50). Renewing banner+top while the banner is already booked into the next season charges the full $75 instead of the $25 difference.
5. **M2. Dispute and evidence readiness is thin.** No dispute due-date is stored or shown, no evidence checklist, no consent to the Terms recorded at checkout, no statement-descriptor suffix set, Radar settings unverified (`docs/CHECKLIST.md:61`), and a refunded order that later "wins" a dispute is relabeled `dispute_won` (reproduced, E4b).

Counts: **High 3, Medium 9, Low 10, Info 3** (plus CPA and attorney question lists in section 4 and a 24-item auto-renewal failure-mode list in section 5).

**Urgent, should not wait for morning:** nothing here is losing money tonight (live revenue is $0 and one installer exists). Two things to decide before anything is merged tonight: (a) do not merge the auto-renewal agent's work without the Terms, Pricing and Sponsor copy changes and a stored consent (H3); (b) before telling any installer to buy, do the $10 live test purchase and look at Stripe's webhook delivery log and Cloudflare security events for `/api/stripe-webhook` (H1).

## 2. Money-path review (what I checked and how it came out)

**Featured events.** Prices come only from `PLANS` (`featured.js:9-13`); `/api/config` live shows 1000, 1500, 2500, `featureOpenDays: 30`, matching code, Terms (`terms.html:56`), Help (`0168`), Pricing and Feature (rendered from config). Window: `applyPlan` (`featured.js:43-56`) is `min(end_date, today + 30/90)`, never shortens, level never lowers. Checked edge cases:
- Event ends today: until = today (one day). Event ended before the webhook: `until = today` (`:52`), so the buyer gets "through today" for a full price. Checkout refuses `end_date < today` but a payment pending across Pacific midnight can still land here. Low.
- Dates change after payment: fixed by design (`featured-window.test.mjs:42`); the owner cannot edit dates (claim gives no edit rights), see M8.
- Cancelled or postponed after payment: flagged in Orders and the digest (`orders.js:12`). Before payment: not blocked server-side (M3).
- Pending submission: paid, held as `sub:<id>`, applied on approval, `refund_due` on decline (`orders.test.mjs:24`), unpublish and delete are guarded with 409 (`:180-206`).
- Full run: 31+ days and 7+ days left (`eventPlanProblem`, `featured.js:32-38`); legacy $20 orders grandfathered (`:8`).
- Staff and ineligible owners: ownership by submission, approved claim or contest owner/staff with no admin note (`ownership.js`); a pending or declined claim cannot buy (`checkout.test.mjs:28`). Banned or deleted accounts: no flag exists to block a payer; deletion keeps orders with `user_id = NULL` (`account.js:51`).
- Duplicate clicks: one session per 10-minute bucket (see L5).

**Featured homes (podium).** `homeForCheckout` reads the frozen results snapshot (`podium.js:78-86`); 30 days from payment, stacking without limit (`applyPlan` with `opts.home`), Full run refused at checkout and ignored by the webhook. `featurableHomes` scans up to 3000 posts per call (`podium.js:64`), fine at 6 homes. No funnel counter (L7).

**Installer sponsor.** Rules are in one tested module (`sponsor.js`). Verified by experiment: season boundaries for 14 dates across the year wrap (Nov 2/3, Jan 6/7, Feb 1/15/16, May 15/16, Aug 31/Sep 1, Dec 26/27, Jan 6 2028) all return the expected window; one-season-ahead horizon, renewal from `cur + 1`, upgrade price `$75 - $50` only when the top card is not running (M2). Refund and dispute recompute from remaining orders (`revokeInstaller`, `sponsor.js:101-117`) and admin date floor (`installerFloor`). Gaps: H2, M1, M2, M4.

**Webhook.** Signature (HMAC SHA-256, 5-minute tolerance, constant-time compare) is correct. Handled events match the Dashboard subscription documented in `stripe-setup.md` (six events). Not handled: `charge.dispute.updated` and `charge.dispute.funds_withdrawn` (informational), `checkout.session.async_payment_failed` (no state change needed), `payment_intent.*` (needed for auto-renewal), `refund.failed` (a failed refund would leave the order marked refunded by an admin while the customer is not refunded; `mark_refunded` does not verify, L9). Out-of-order delivery: refund or dispute first is stored and applied (tested); `async_payment_succeeded` after `completed unpaid` works. Webhook retries: 500 for recoverable failures; the pre-check at `:24` is read-then-write (L2).

**Held payments.** Unapproved event: webhook returns `held`, buyer gets the "we are checking it first" email (`featuremail.js`), digest flags after 24 hours (`orders.js:10`). Admin approve applies orders one at a time. OK.

**Refunds, partial refunds, disputes.** Full refund revokes via recompute; partial holds with a note; dispute created revokes; lost stays revoked; won becomes `dispute_won` and stays off (by design). New observation: E4b, a refunded order later marked won becomes `dispute_won`, hiding the refund in the status column (M5).

**Admin Orders and digest.** `NEEDS_ATTENTION` (`orders.js:9-12`) covers refund due, disputed, held, unapplied, `[auto]` notes, placement on cancelled events; the digest reports it three times a day and failure of the digest opens a GitHub issue (`submission-digest.yml`). What is missing: orders that never arrived (H1), revenue totals and a CSV, and anything about Stripe-side state.

**Emails.** `feature_paid`, `sponsor_paid`, `sponsor_admin` are best effort and never block the placement; a send failure is logged to `ops_log` via `sendMail`. With Stripe down at checkout the buyer gets a friendly 502 and nothing is written (`stripe.js:17-21`). With Stripe down at refund time the owner refunds by hand; nothing in the app calls Stripe except the dispute charge lookup (`stripe-webhook.js:57`). With email down the digest does not set `last_at` so it retries (`admin-digest.js:53`).

**Funnel counters.** Server-side counters cannot be forged (`event-funnel.test.mjs:106`); numbers only; homes and refunds uncounted (L7).

## 5. Auto-renewal for installers: what the builder must handle

No design doc exists on `origin/main` at audit time, so these are the assumptions I could infer from the current code and the failure modes I would require. Facts the builder starts from: Checkout is guest-based (no Stripe Customer), order primary key is the Checkout session id, `paid()` handles only Checkout events, the webhook subscription lists six events, the only scheduler is GitHub Actions calling a key-protected endpoint (`submission-digest.yml`, `uptime.yml`), the digest and Admin > Orders only see D1 rows.

**Consent and copy**
1. Explicit opt-in at purchase (unchecked checkbox or Checkout consent), amount, period, next charge date and how to cancel, stored with timestamp, Terms version, user id and plan; Terms, Pricing, Sponsor, Help and the receipt email updated first (H3).
2. Pre-renewal reminder email (how many days before is an attorney question), receipt email after each charge, failure email with a pay-now link, cancellation confirmation. Fail closed: if the reminder cannot be sent, do not charge.
3. A price-change rule: what happens when `INSTALLER_PLANS` changes between consent and charge (notice, or lock).

**Saving the card**
4. Checkout must create a Customer (`customer_creation=always`) and `payment_intent_data[setup_future_usage]=off_session`; today it passes only `customer_email`. Cash App Pay and other non-card methods may not be reusable off-session: restrict to cards for the renewal opt-in.
5. Store only Stripe ids (customer, payment method) in D1; account deletion must detach or delete them (`account.js` deletion list) and turn renewals off; update `privacy.html`.

**Charging**
6. Idempotency: one claimed row per (installer, plan, target season) with a unique constraint written before the Stripe call, plus a Stripe `idempotency-key` on the PaymentIntent create. A cron overlap, a retry after a timeout, or a manual run must not charge twice.
7. Unknown outcome (timeout, 5xx): retrieve the PaymentIntent by the stored key before retrying; never create a second one.
8. Charge succeeds but our apply fails (Worker error, D1 down): the attempt row exists in `pending` state, so a reconcile job and the existing "paid but not applied" flag (`orders.js:10`) can see it. Today an order row only appears after a successful Checkout webhook.
9. New webhook events (`payment_intent.succeeded`, `payment_intent.payment_failed`, `payment_intent.requires_action`) need to be added to `HANDLED` (`stripe-webhook.js:16`) and subscribed in the Stripe Dashboard by the owner (document it in `stripe-setup.md`). Checkout-created PaymentIntents also emit `payment_intent.succeeded`: distinguish by metadata so a Checkout payment is never applied twice.
10. Order key: `feature_orders.session_id` is a primary key and `paid()` and `applyOrder` use it; renewal orders need a stable synthetic id (the PaymentIntent id) so refund, dispute and pending-revocation paths (`orderByPi`) keep working.
11. Recompute at charge time with the same `sponsorWindow` and compare with the amount charged (M1 shows the webhook-time recompute pattern); the one-season-ahead horizon (`full`) must be re-checked so a manual renewal made after consent does not stack a second season.
12. Pacific time only: decide the charge date relative to the season end in Pacific (H2, L1), including DST and the Jan 6 / Jan 7 boundary. Consider that auto-renewing Christmas into New Year's Jan 7 to 31 would charge a full $50 for 25 low-value days right after the only season that matters (M7): the renewal should probably skip low-value seasons or ask.

**Failures**
13. Declines, expired card, insufficient funds, `authentication_required` (3-D Secure off-session): email with a hosted pay-now link, retry rules within card-network limits, a lapse date, no retry loop. Admin > Orders and the digest must list failed renewals.
14. Race with manual checkout, cancel, hide, delete, merge, ownership change (`ownsInstaller` at charge time), profile hidden by admin (`status != 'active'` means do not charge), and the admin date floor (`installerFloor`).
15. Refund or lost dispute of a renewal must also turn auto-renewal off for that installer; a disputing customer must never be charged again.
16. Cancellation honored up to the moment of charge (check the flag in the same statement that claims the attempt row), one click in the dashboard, plus by email.
17. Stripe down at charge time: leave the attempt `pending`, alert via `ops_log`, retry the next day within the grace period. Email down: see 2.
18. The scheduler: GitHub Actions cron is best-effort (delays, skipped runs, and scheduled workflows in a repository without activity are disabled after a period); a missed run means silent lapse. Make the job idempotent and catch-up capable, add a "last renewal run" heartbeat to the digest, and alert when a renewal due date passes without an attempt.
19. In-app wrapper: the paid flow is web-only (`sponsor/checkout.js:14`); saved-card management must be hidden in the apps too.

**Visibility and QA**
20. Admin > Orders: renewal orders with their own flag, a list of upcoming renewals with dates and amounts, a monthly revenue report.
21. Test with Stripe test clocks and with the in-memory D1 harness (`tests/_pay.mjs`): double run, timeout then retry, decline then success, cancel race, refund of renewal, dispute of renewal, two seasons booked, hidden profile, deleted account.
22. Dispute evidence for renewals: stored consent, reminder email log (we do not log sent emails today), receipt, `banner_stats`.
23. Consider Stripe Billing (subscriptions with dunning, 3-D Secure flow and customer portal) against a home-grown cron: variable season end dates fit Billing poorly, but dunning, SCA and the portal are what the home-grown version must rebuild.
24. Tax and legal: section 4 attorney item 1; confirm whether renewal receipts must show anything additional.

## 6b. Fraud, abuse and monitoring

- Card testing: Hosted Checkout (Stripe Radar applies) behind sign-in, same-origin, ownership and the 6-an-hour and 20-a-day account cap (`stripe.js:6-14`, tested `checkout.test.mjs:139`). Remaining: accounts are cheap, no IP cap (L6), Radar rules unverified, no alert on 3 or more failed payments an hour (Stripe emails the account owner only for its own default rules).
- Ineligible owners and impersonation: ownership rules are strict (`ownership.js`); claim auto-approval hardening from the earlier audit (P11) is separate.
- Chargeback risk: descriptor not set (M5); consumer-looking one-time purchases of $10 to $25; Terms refund text is clear. Sponsor buyers are businesses.
- Monitoring: `ops_log` plus the three-times-a-day digest (webhook 5xx, email failures, orders needing a human) and uptime workflow every 15 minutes. Blind spots: bad signature (H1), a webhook that never arrives (H1), Stripe outage, failed refund, GitHub schedule disabled (digest has an "Admin digest failed" issue, `submission-digest.yml`, but a schedule that stops running does not open one).

## 7. Revenue model sanity (real numbers only)

- Docs baseline (2026-11-04): $0 ever paid on Stripe, 1 expired $5 checkout, 0 orders, 6 homes, 1 installer profile, 1 contest, 5 accounts, humans 20 to 50 a day; Finance estimate for Nov 4 to Jan 2 is $0 to $300 (`finance-pricing.md` section 1). The one live banner today is "Lights Up Today" (`GET /api/banner`), set by hand.
- What the new sponsor prices change: one installer paying $50 or $75 plus the $25 upgrade is the ceiling this season ($50 net $48.25; $75 net $72.52). The sponsor funnel counters (`fn:sponsor-*`) are the gate.
- Per-installer annual ceiling at list price with every season bought: Banner 7 seasons x $50 = $350 (Christmas, New Year's, Valentine's, Spring, Summer, Halloween plus renewals; at most one season ahead), banner+top $525; realistic demand is Christmas and Halloween only. Net of fees about 3.5% to 4%.
- Fixed costs are about $0 (`finance-pricing.md` section 2), so any sale is profit before time; the cost of a lost dispute on a $10 pin exceeds two sales. With revenue this small, reconciliation cost matters more than rate: an hour a month of manual Stripe CSV work (`finance-operations.md` section 3) is the real expense, which is why H1 matters.
