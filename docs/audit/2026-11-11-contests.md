# Audit 2026-11-11: contests, every role

Read-only audit. Scope: organizer, resident, voter, judge, results (`/past`, `/api/archive`), `/contests`, Founder city badge, passes, as they affect contests. No product code changed.

**How it was tested**
- **Local** `wrangler pages dev` on a copy of `origin/main` (9497b08), migrations applied, tutorial demo seed (`scripts/tutorials/seed-demo.mjs`, `seed-founder.sql`), session cookies for the demo accounts, `DEV_AUTH`. Playwright/Chromium at 440x956 DPR 2 (iPhone Pro Max class), 360x640, 1280x900; light and dark; keyboard Tab walk; axe-core 4 on 9 pages in both themes; user agent `SoCalLightsApp/1.0`. Everything that signs in or submits (entry, votes, judge score, wizard, announce) ran only against the local copy. I also announced the local demo contest, so results pages were seen from real frozen output.
- **Live** (`socallights.app`): anonymous GET browsing only (contests list, the one live contest, `/past`, `/c/nope`, `/pricing`, `/contest-pass`, `/api/contests`, `/api/config`). No sign-in, no form, vote, payment or Turnstile click. A fake browser clock (client side only) was used to test the time-of-day behaviour.
- Each finding is tagged **[T]** confirmed by testing or **[R]** from reading code (not reproduced). Fixture note: the local data was seeded for tutorial videos, so counts such as "27 flagged" are partly a seed artefact; flagged where relevant.
- Owner-accepted items (red "Ask SoCal Lights" circle, focus leaving the Route sheet, first-visit California zoom) are deliberately not reported.

## Summary: top 10 issues
1. **CON-01** Judge score box is squeezed to 64px on phones and shows "S" or "Sc" instead of the 1-10 score. The main judging control is hard to read on the device judges will use. [T]
2. **CON-02** Judge landing page has no "Start scoring" button; categories live only in the bottom bar and the third category hides under "More". [T]
3. **CON-03** A home can win "Judges' Choice" on one score from one judge for one home while 20 homes have "no scores"; the result page shows it as an ordinary award. [T]
4. **CON-04** After winners are announced the organizer dashboard still offers "Open entries" and "Show counts" (cannot work), still says "4 entries need your OK", and the poster still says "Scan to enter. Enter by Dec 10". No "announced, what now" state. [T]
5. **CON-05** The contest page decides "Voting opens" using the UTC date. From 4 pm Pacific on the day before voting the pill flips from "Voting opens Dec 12" to "Voting not open". [T on live with fake clock]
6. **CON-06** Contest pass prices ($149 / $299 / $499) are not on `/pricing` or `/contest-pass` for signed-out visitors. A city clerk first sees a price after completing the whole wizard. [T on live]
7. **CON-07** Review screen says "Your contest goes live right away" directly above "You have no pass running" and a three-card $149 picker; the "Create contest" button sits below and only then errors. [T]
8. **CON-08** No page states a time or time zone for "last day to enter", "voting opens" or "voting closes" (grep: "Pacific" appears on no contest-facing page). [T/R]
9. **CON-09** Ties are listed as plain separate winners (three "Most Creative: People's Choice" rows); results can be misread. [T]
10. **CON-10** Live Covina contest slug ends in a dash (`...holiday-home-lighting-`, 40-char cut), and that URL is what the QR code and posters carry. [T on live]

## Confirmed by testing vs from reading
- Tested: CON-01 to 05, 06, 07, 09 to 18, 22 (with fixture), 23 text, 25, 27, 28, 29 (app UA), 30, 31. Parts from reading: CON-08 rule logic, CON-19 reasoning, CON-20, CON-21 mail list, CON-24, CON-26.
- Not tested (state it plainly): real email delivery (no Resend key locally, outbox failed for "results_entrant" in dev), Stripe checkout, Google sign-in, the unpublish/publish-again click path, entry withdrawal click path, Turnstile, real address autocomplete, tablet width. I did not exercise the live site beyond anonymous GETs.

## What works well
- Golden paths complete end to end: Quick start wizard (7 screens, defaults sensible), comp user creates a live contest with no payment and gets a clear "What happens next".
- Vote rules: one vote per category, moving a vote works, own home disabled ("Your home"), progress "Your ballot: voted in 1 of 3", category tabs with checkmarks.
- Pass picker in the app wrapper shows no price or Stripe; web shows all three plans; no "Everything in..." phrasing.
- Announce asks for confirmation twice and warns when a judge has not finished; results freeze and appear on `/past` with every home, votes, judge averages and Founder city badge.
- Closed or missing contests fail clearly ("We can't find that contest", "Entries are closed"), no horizontal scroll at 360, first Tab stop is "Skip to the content", axe finds no violations on the entry, wizard, dashboard, judge, `/past` and `/contest-pass` pages in light mode.
