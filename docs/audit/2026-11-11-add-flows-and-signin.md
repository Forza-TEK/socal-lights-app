# Audit: add flows and sign-in (2026-11-11)

Area: /add, /add-event, /add-house (wizard), sign-in sheet, account menu, profile (Drafts / Displays / Events), edit and delete, photo upload, drafts, emails, installer pages.
Auditor: add-flows. Read-only: no product code changed.

## How it was tested

- **Confirmed by testing** means reproduced in Chromium (Playwright) against a local `wrangler pages dev` copy of origin/main (9497b08) with the local D1 database migrated, the tutorial demo seed applied, and `DEV_AUTH=1` (emailed code handed back on screen). Viewports: iPhone 17 Pro Max 440x956 @3x, small phone 360x640 @2x, tablet 820x1180, desktop 1440x900; light and dark; keyboard Tab/Esc; offline and mocked 429/500; double click on submit; reload mid-wizard; the in-app user agent (`SoCalLightsApp`).
- **From reading code** means I found it in the source and did not (or could not) reproduce it. Labelled in the table.
- The live site was only used for anonymous GET of `/add`, `/add-event`, `/add-house` (status 200, titles). No sign-in, no submit, no Turnstile click on live.
- Not testable here: Google sign-in (no Google keys locally), the Cloudflare Turnstile widget (the sandbox blocks challenges.cloudflare.com, the widget showed "Unable to connect to website"), real email delivery, Capacitor device behavior (only the user-agent branch was exercised), HEIC photos (Chromium here cannot make one).
- Screenshots: `docs/audit/screens/add/` (13 JPEGs, local copy, demo data only).

## Summary: top 10 issues

1. **ADD-01 (P1)** Photos silently disappear on reload or on "Save & exit", and the review screen only says "Photos 0". Only name/text answers are kept.
2. **ADD-02 (P1)** The owner cannot add or change photos, address or season after posting. The edit sheet has none of them, and the photo step tells people to "add them later by email".
3. **ADD-03 (P1)** Nobody gets a "we received it" email, and a home that goes live at once gets no email at all. The finished screen does not say that either, or link to My homes/Edit.
4. **ADD-04 (P1)** Duplicate event result contradicts itself: heading "this event is already on the map", body "It is on the map shortly.", and it offers "Feature your event" for an event that may belong to someone else.
5. **ADD-05 (P1)** The sign-in sheet is not a real dialog: no focus trap (12 Tabs left the sheet), no focus return, errors not announced, empty email is sent to the server before any message.
6. **ADD-06 (P1)** After a validation error focus stays on the Next button. Screen reader and keyboard users are not taken to the field. Only the first empty field is flagged, and never a too-short one.
7. **ADD-07 (P1)** Network failure shows the raw browser text "Failed to fetch"; the rate limit message ("Try again in an hour") is a dead end for shared Wi-Fi with no way to save or contact anyone.
8. **ADD-08 (P1)** Word mix: "display" in almost every place (tiles, titles, profile tab, emails, buttons), "house" in the "About this house" label and in the page title "Show your lights", "Homes" in the installer area. The product word is "home".
9. **ADD-09 (P1)** Abandoned drafts reopen days later at the old step (often Review) with no "Start over", and are shared by everyone who uses the browser (not tied to the account, not cleared at sign-out).
10. **ADD-10 (P2)** Profile opens on the empty "Drafts" tab right after you posted something; your new home is two taps away under "Displays". Delete is a two-tap with no description of what is lost.

## What already works well

- Signed-in account menu is accessible: button has `aria-haspopup="menu"` and `aria-expanded`, items have `role=menuitem`, focus moves in, Esc closes.
- Sign-in sheet fields are correct: `type=email inputmode=email autocomplete=email`, code field `autocomplete=one-time-code inputmode=numeric`, clear "No password" line, 13+/18+ age note, helpful wrong-code message.
- Address fields have `autocomplete` (address-line1, address-level2, postal-code), ZIP numeric keypad; inline validation messages are plain and specific ("Add your street address.", "The ZIP code needs 5 digits.").
- Double-submit guarded: two clicks sent one POST (`POSTs sent: 1`), button reads "Sending…" and is disabled.
- Save & exit round trip works (draft shows under Saved drafts with a Continue link); "Take down for the season" is reversible with clear wording; leave dialog "Save your progress?" is clear.
- No horizontal overflow at 360, 440, 820, 1440, light or dark; dark theme has readable error colour; the live preview card on desktop is a nice touch; the in-app user agent hides Google as designed.
- Photos shrink on device in about 220 ms for a 12 MP image; text and fake images get plain messages.
