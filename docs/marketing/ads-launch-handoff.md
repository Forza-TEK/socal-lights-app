# Ads launch handoff (Facebook and Instagram only)

Paste into a Claude in Chrome session or give to Grok. The owner is signed in to Meta.

## Hard rules
- STOP and ask the owner before anything that spends money or publishes: before Publish, Submit for review, Confirm or Turn on. Show the owner the campaign summary first (name, budget, dates, daily cap, audience, ads).
- Facebook and Instagram (Meta) only. Do NOT create anything in Google Ads or Nextdoor.
- Budget: $280 is loaded across all Meta campaigns below ($20 reserve is held back and not loaded). Set the Meta account spending limit to $280. Every ad ends by Dec 8, 2026. Never raise a budget, change a payment method or add a card.
- Use lifetime budgets with an end date for each ad set, equal to the "Budget" and "Dates" shown; the daily figure is only for checking the pace.
- Pacific Time. Target by county only (Los Angeles, Orange, San Diego, Riverside, San Bernardino, Ventura, Santa Barbara). No city or ZIP targeting.
- Placements: Facebook feed, Instagram feed, Instagram Stories and Reels (Advantage+ placements is fine). Connect the Instagram business account to the SoCalLights.app Facebook Page so ads show under the SoCal Lights name.
- Use ONLY the text below, word for word. No new ad copy, claims, prices or images. Use 'home', never 'house' or 'display'.
- Do not run: ev-c-*, in-c-*, fa-d-*, fa-e-*, fa-f-*, newsletter items, or any Google or Nextdoor item.
- Do not enter passwords, 2-factor codes or card numbers; the owner does that.
- If an identity check or policy flag appears, or an ad is disapproved, stop and tell the owner.
- Links already contain tracking codes (ref and utm). Do not edit them.
