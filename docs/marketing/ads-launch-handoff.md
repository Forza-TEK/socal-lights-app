# Ads launch handoff (for a Claude in Chrome session)

Paste this file into a Claude session on the owner's computer that has Claude in Chrome. The owner is signed in to Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google Ads and Nextdoor Business. Work in new tabs.

## Hard rules
- STOP and ask the owner before anything that spends money or publishes: before clicking Publish, Submit for review, Confirm, or Turn on. Show the owner the campaign summary first (name, budget, dates, daily cap, audience, ads).
- Total spend for all ads is $300 and every ad ends by Dec 8, 2026. Never raise a budget, change a payment method, or add a card.
- Target by county only (Los Angeles, Orange, San Diego, Riverside, San Bernardino, Ventura, Santa Barbara). Do not target a single city or ZIP code.
- Use ONLY the text below, word for word. Do not write new ad copy, claims, prices or images. Use 'home', never 'house' or 'display'.
- Do not run: ev-c-*, in-c-*, fa-d-*, fa-f-*, newsletter items, or the installer Google ads. Meta account spending limit: $160.
- Do not enter passwords, 2-factor codes or card numbers; the owner does that.
- If a page asks to confirm identity, a policy flag appears, or an ad is disapproved, stop and tell the owner.
- Landing links already contain the tracking codes (ref and utm). Do not edit them.
