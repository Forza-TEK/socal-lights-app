# City coverage checklist

The weekly deep search splits Southern California into these regions and checks **every city** in a
region, one by one (city parks & recreation / community services calendar, downtown association,
chamber of commerce, malls, local news). A region's helper reports any city where it found nothing,
so gaps are visible instead of silent.

## 1. Los Angeles – Central, Westside & Valley
Los Angeles (Downtown, Hollywood, Silver Lake/Echo Park, Eastside/Boyle Heights, Highland Park, Griffith Park),
Beverly Hills, West Hollywood, Culver City, Santa Monica, Malibu, Calabasas, Agoura Hills, Westlake Village,
Hidden Hills, Burbank, Glendale, La Cañada Flintridge, San Fernando, and LA's San Fernando Valley
neighborhoods (Encino, Sherman Oaks, Studio City, North Hollywood, Van Nuys, Woodland Hills, Northridge,
Granada Hills, Chatsworth, Sylmar, Tujunga).

## 2. Los Angeles – San Gabriel & Pomona Valleys
Pasadena, Altadena, South Pasadena, San Marino, Arcadia, Monrovia, Sierra Madre, Duarte, Azusa, Glendora,
San Dimas, La Verne, Claremont, Pomona, Diamond Bar, Walnut, West Covina, Covina, Baldwin Park, Irwindale,
El Monte, South El Monte, Temple City, Rosemead, San Gabriel, Alhambra, Monterey Park, Montebello,
Rowland Heights, Hacienda Heights, La Puente, Industry.

## 3. Los Angeles – South Bay, Long Beach, Gateway, North County
Long Beach, Lakewood, Signal Hill, Torrance, Redondo Beach, Hermosa Beach, Manhattan Beach, El Segundo,
Palos Verdes Estates, Rancho Palos Verdes, Rolling Hills Estates, San Pedro, Carson, Gardena, Hawthorne,
Inglewood, Lawndale, Downey, Norwalk, Whittier, La Mirada, Cerritos, Bellflower, Paramount, Pico Rivera,
Santa Fe Springs, South Gate, Lynwood, Compton, Huntington Park, Commerce, Avalon (Catalina),
Santa Clarita (Valencia, Newhall, Saugus, Canyon Country), Lancaster, Palmdale.

## 4. Orange County
Aliso Viejo, Anaheim, Brea, Buena Park, Costa Mesa, Cypress, Dana Point, Fountain Valley, Fullerton,
Garden Grove, Huntington Beach, Irvine, La Habra, La Palma, Laguna Beach, Laguna Hills, Laguna Niguel,
Laguna Woods, Lake Forest, Los Alamitos, Mission Viejo, Newport Beach, Orange, Placentia,
Rancho Santa Margarita, San Clemente, San Juan Capistrano, Santa Ana, Seal Beach, Stanton, Tustin,
Villa Park, Westminster, Yorba Linda, plus Ladera Ranch, Coto de Caza, Trabuco Canyon, Rossmoor.

## 5. San Diego County
San Diego (Downtown, Little Italy, Old Town, Balboa Park, Point Loma, Mission Bay, Pacific Beach, La Jolla,
North Park, Barrio Logan, Clairemont, Rancho Bernardo), Carlsbad, Oceanside, Vista, San Marcos, Escondido,

La Mesa, Lemon Grove, Lakeside, Alpine, Chula Vista, National City, Imperial Beach, Coronado, Bonita, Borrego Springs.

## 6. Riverside & San Bernardino counties
Riverside: Riverside, Corona, Norco, Eastvale, Jurupa Valley, Moreno Valley, Perris, Menifee, Lake Elsinore,
Wildomar, Murrieta, Temecula, Hemet, San Jacinto, Beaumont, Banning, Palm Springs, Cathedral City,
Rancho Mirage, Palm Desert, Indian Wells, La Quinta, Indio, Coachella.

## 7. Ventura & Santa Barbara counties
Ventura: Ventura, Oxnard, Port Hueneme, Camarillo, Thousand Oaks, Newbury Park, Westlake Village, Simi Valley,
Moorpark, Ojai, Santa Paula, Fillmore.
Santa Barbara: Santa Barbara, Montecito, Goleta, Carpinteria, Solvang, Buellton, Los Olivos, Santa Ynez,
Lompoc, Santa Maria, Orcutt, Guadalupe.
