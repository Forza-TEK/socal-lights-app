      Good to know

      Is it labeled as an ad?

      Featured events carry a visible Featured badge, so nobody is misled. We only list real, public events, and a Featured event still has to be accurate: dates, venue and address are checked like every other listing.

      What can I feature?

      Public Halloween, Día de los Muertos, Thanksgiving, Christmas and holiday, New Year's, spring, and summer events in Southern California: haunts, pumpkin patches, tree lightings, light shows, parades, markets and more. We reserve the right to decline events that are not open to the public or that we cannot verify.

      Can I get a refund?

      If we cannot feature your event for any reason, you get a full refund. Once your placement is live, payments are non-refundable, but if your event is cancelled we will move your placement to another event of yours or refund the unused time. A refund switches your Featured placement off, and so does a payment dispute, so please email us before disputing a charge with your bank.

      Does Featured change my event's details?

      No. Your event keeps its dates, hours and link. Send us corrections any time at no charge.
