    Good to know

    Is it labeled as an ad?

    Featured events and homes carry a visible Featured badge, and an event's banner pill at the top of the map list is labelled "Featured", so nobody is misled. We only list real, public events, and a Featured event still has to be accurate: dates, venue and address are checked like every other listing.

    What can I feature?

    Public Halloween, Día de los Muertos, Thanksgiving, Christmas and holiday, New Year's, spring, and summer events in Southern California: haunts, pumpkin patches, tree lightings, light shows, parades, markets and more. We reserve the right to decline events that are not open to the public or that we cannot verify.

    Can I get a refund?

    If we cannot list or feature your event for any reason, you get a full refund, for every part of your plan: the pin, the banner and top of the list. Once your placement is live, payments are non-refundable, but if your event is cancelled, email us and we will move your placement to another of your events or refund the unused time. A refund switches your Featured placement off (the pin, the banner and top of the list), and so does a payment dispute, so please email info@socallights.app before disputing a charge with your bank. The same rules are in our Terms.

    How long does it run, and what does an upgrade cost?

    It runs from the moment you pay until your event ends or the season ends (Christmas lights: Jan 6), whichever comes first, and the price is the same whenever you buy. Each part (the Featured pin, the banner, top of the list) has the same price: if you already have some of them, you pay only for the parts you add, and a purchase that adds nothing new is not sold. This page shows the exact dates and the total before you pay.

    Does Featured change my event's details?

    No. Your event keeps its dates, hours and link. Send us corrections any time at no charge.
