Good to know

What does "free for now" mean?

It is free today. We may add paid options later, and this page always shows the current prices.

Will finding lights ever cost money?

Finding events and lights, search, routes, favorites and voting in contests are meant to stay free for everyone. That is what the site is for.

Can paying change who wins a contest?

No. Contests are free to enter and free to vote in, and payment never affects who wins or any contest result. A home can only be featured after the winners are announced, and only if it finished winner, 2nd or 3rd place; places come from the results as they were locked at the announcement.

Is a Featured event labelled?

Yes. Featured events and homes carry a visible Featured badge so nobody is misled, and they still have to be real, public and accurate.
