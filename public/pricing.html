Good to know

What does "season" mean?

A placement runs from the moment you pay until your event ends or the season ends (Christmas lights: Jan 6), whichever comes first. The price is the same whenever you buy in a season, so the exact end date is shown before you pay.

How do I pay, and do I get a receipt?

You pay on Stripe's secure page with a card or Cash App Pay. Stripe emails the receipt, and we send a confirmation. There is no subscription for event plans.

Can I get a refund?

If we cannot list or feature your event for any reason, you get a full refund. Once your placement is live, payments are non-refundable, but if your event is cancelled, email us and we will move your placement to another of your events or refund the unused time. See our Terms.

Can I add a part later?

Yes. Each part (the Featured pin, the banner, top of the list) has the same price: if you already have some of them, you pay only for the parts you add.

Can paying change who wins a contest?

No. Contests are free to enter and free to vote in, and payment never affects who wins or any contest result. A home can only be featured after the winners are announced, and only if it finished winner, 2nd or 3rd place.

Is a Featured event labelled?

Yes. Featured events and homes carry a visible Featured badge, and the banner pill is labelled "Featured", so nobody is misled. They still have to be real, public and accurate.

How do contest passes work?

An organization gets one free contest in each 12-month window. The window starts the day its first contest goes live. To run more contests in that window, it buys a pass for a total number of contests (the free one included), and can upgrade later by paying only the difference. A contest counts when it goes live. Drafts, contests held for review and contests that were never live do not count. When the window ends, your next contest starts a new window and is free again. A pass counts contests only: entering and voting stay free, and payment never affects any result.

Will finding lights ever cost money?

Finding events and lights, search, routes, favorites and voting in contests are meant to stay free for everyone. That is what the site is for.
