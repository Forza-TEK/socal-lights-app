Pricing

SoCal Lights is a free map of holiday lights and events across Southern California. Here is what is free and what is optional, so you can see it before you add anything.

Finding lights and events: free

Searching the map, filters, routes, saving favorites, the Help Center, and voting in contests are free for everyone. No account is needed to browse.

Featured events and podium homes (optional)

A Featured event stands out with a gold Featured badge and a bigger gold-ringed pin on the map. It is the only thing on SoCal Lights that costs money today.

Homes can be featured too, but only by the owner of a home that finished winner, 2nd or 3rd place in an announced contest, for the same 30-day plans (no season plan). Payment never changes who wins or any contest result.

Prices are per event or per home. You pay on Stripe's secure page. Featured placements are always labelled. Feature my event

Good to know

What does "free for now" mean?

It is free today. We may add paid options later, and this page always shows the current prices.

Will finding lights ever cost money?

Finding events and lights, search, routes, favorites and voting in contests are meant to stay free for everyone. That is what the site is for.

Can paying change who wins a contest?

No. Contests are free to enter and free to vote in, and payment never affects who wins or any contest result. A home can only be featured after the winners are announced, and only if it finished winner, 2nd or 3rd place; places come from the results as they were locked at the announcement.

Is a Featured event labelled?

Yes. Featured events and homes carry a visible Featured badge so nobody is misled, and they still have to be real, public and accurate.
