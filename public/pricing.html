Contest organizers

Contest organizers choose a pass for their contests. Cities, homeowners associations (HOAs), chambers of commerce and neighborhood groups: resident entries, voting, judges, and People's Choice and Judges' Choice awards. Residents enter and vote for free. A pass only counts contests.

Contest passes are bought on socallights.app in a browser, not inside the app.

Passes are chosen when you create a contest, or any time on the contest pass page. A pass is one payment on Stripe's secure page and never renews by itself. Your 12-month window starts the day you buy your pass (or the day your first contest goes live, if that comes first), and upgrading inside it costs only the difference. Passes are for the organization: the website matching your email's domain, or your own account.

Good to know

What does "season" mean?

A placement runs from the moment you pay until your event ends or the season ends (Christmas lights: Jan 6), whichever comes first. The price is the same whenever you buy in a season, so the exact end date is shown before you pay.

How do I pay, and do I get a receipt?

You pay on Stripe's secure page. You can pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Amazon Pay, Cash App Pay or Link. Which options you see depends on your device and location. Stripe emails the receipt, and we send a confirmation. There is no subscription for event plans. The price you see is the price you pay. We do not add sales tax. Prices are in US dollars.

Can I get a refund?

If we cannot list or feature your event for any reason, you get a full refund. Once your placement is live it is non-refundable, but if your event is cancelled we will move it to another of your events or refund the unused time. See our Terms.

Can I add a part later?

Yes. Each part (the Featured pin, the banner, top of the list) has the same price: if you already have some of them, you pay only for the parts you add.

Can paying change who wins a contest?

No. Contests are free to enter and free to vote in, and payment never affects who wins or any contest result. A home can only be featured after the winners are announced, and only if it finished winner, 2nd or 3rd place.

Is a Featured event labeled?

Yes. Featured events and homes carry a visible Featured badge, and the banner pill is labeled "Featured", so nobody is misled. They still have to be real, public and accurate.

How do contest passes work?

An organization chooses a pass before a contest goes live: a single contest, up to 5 contests or up to 20 contests in a 12-month window. The window starts the day you buy the pass, or the day the organization's first contest goes live if that comes first. You can upgrade later by paying only the difference. A contest counts when it goes live. Drafts, contests held for review and contests that were never live do not count. When the window ends, your next contest needs a new pass. A pass counts contests only: entering and voting stay free, and payment never affects any result. We sometimes give a founding partner contests for a period; that organization sees "Founding partner: free through" a date and no price.

Will finding lights ever cost money?

Finding events and lights, search, routes, favorites and voting in contests are meant to stay free for everyone. That is what the site is for.
