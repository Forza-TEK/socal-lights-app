Paid placements

Finding lights and events, listing an event or a home, entering a contest and voting are free. Paid placements are optional and only make a listing easier to see. Prices and the exact start and end dates are shown on our Pricing page and on the Feature page before you pay.

  • What it is. A Featured placement gives an event or a home a gold-ringed pin on the map and a "Featured" badge in the list and on its details. For an event it is sold in parts: the Featured pin (always included in any paid plan), a banner (the event shows as a pill labelled "Featured" in the strip at the top of the map list, with its name, city and dates, while it is in the map view) and top of the list (the event is listed first when people browse the area). The plans are the pin, the banner with the pin, top of the list with the pin, and all three, at the prices on our Pricing page. A home can be featured with the pin or with top of the list.
  • How you pay. You pay once, by card on Stripe's payment page. There is no subscription and nothing renews by itself. We never see your card details. Stripe emails your receipt.
  • Always labelled. A paid placement always carries the "Featured" badge, and an event's banner pill is labelled "Featured" (for installers, the "Sponsor" label described below). A paid placement does not change the details of the listing, and the listing still has to be real and accurate.
  • Contests. Payment never affects any contest result or any vote. A home can be featured only after the contest winners are announced, only if it finished winner, 2nd or 3rd place, and only by the home's owner (or, for a home the organizer added, the organizer who runs that contest).

For an event it ends on the event's last day or on the last day of the season it was bought in (Christmas lights: Jan 6), whichever comes first. The price is flat: it does not depend on when you buy within the season, and the exact start and end dates and the total are shown before you pay. If you already have part of a plan, you pay only for the parts you add, and a purchase that adds nothing new is not sold. For a home it runs for 30 days from payment. Featuring opens 30 days before an event starts, and an event that has ended, been cancelled or been postponed cannot be featured. Buying again adds time and never shortens a placement you already have.

  • No guarantee of results. We do not promise any number of views, visits, clicks or other results from a placement.
