SoCal Lights

Privacy Policy

SoCal Lights (socallights.app), operated by Forza-TEK. Effective October 30, 2026.

SoCal Lights is a free map of Halloween, Christmas and other seasonal events and neighbor-decorated homes in Southern California. You do not need an account to browse the map or add an event; you only sign in to run, enter or vote in a home decorating contest. This page explains what information the website and the SoCal Lights apps handle, and what they do not.

What we do not do

  • We do not use passwords, and you never need an account to browse the map or add an event.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Your location

If you tap the location button, or choose "My location" as the starting point for a route, your device may ask to share its location. Your location is used only on your device, to center the map and to estimate drive times. It is not sent to our servers or stored. You can decline, and everything still works. When you open a route, your maps app (Google Maps or Apple Maps) receives the destinations, under its own privacy policy.

Information you choose to submit

If you use Add an event or Showcase a house, we receive what you type in the form:

  • Events: event name, web page link, dates, hours, price, venue, street address, description and your email address.
  • Home displays: your name or business name, email address, street address, display description and photos. Professional installers may also add a website, phone number and service area.

The event or home details, including the street address and photos, are shown publicly on the map. Your email address is private and is used only to contact you if something needs fixing. We store submissions and photos with Cloudflare. To ask for your event or house to be edited or removed, email info@socallights.app.

Accounts, contests and voting

Creating a home decorating contest, entering a home in one, or voting in one requires signing in. You can sign in with Google or with a one-time code that we email to you. We never ask you to create a password.

  • What we keep: your email address, your name if Google shares it, and your sign-in sessions. If you use Google, we receive only your email address and name; we do not receive your Google password and do not read any other Google data.
  • Sign-in cookie: a cookie keeps you signed in on your device for up to 30 days. Signing out removes it.
  • Contest organizers can see the name, email address, street address and photos of people who enter their contest, so they can run it.
  • Votes are stored with your account so that each person can vote once. They are not shown publicly with your name, and organizers see vote totals, not who voted for whom.
  • Deleting your account: open your profile, choose the Account tab and tap Delete my account. We delete your account, saved drafts, profile picture, contest entries and votes, close any contests you run, and remove your email from homes and events you posted. You can also email info@socallights.app from the address you signed in with.
  • Optional profile details: you can add a name, a home address and a profile picture to your profile. They are used only to fill in forms for you and to show your picture to you, and are never shown publicly. If you sign in with Google we save the picture Google provides until you upload your own.

Spam protection and technical data

Forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from bots. To limit abuse, we briefly record the network (IP) address of each submission, and of each sign-in code we send, and delete it within a day or two. Like any website, our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard connection data such as IP address and browser type to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Information stored on your device

The site remembers a few choices on your device only: your saved places in My night, your day or night display choice, your preferred maps app, and whether you have hidden the My night tips. You can clear these by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Links to other sites and affiliate links

Event pages, ticket sellers, maps apps and Amazon are separate services with their own privacy policies. Some links in the Shop section are Amazon affiliate links: if you buy something, we may earn a small commission at no cost to you. Amazon, not SoCal Lights, receives any information from that visit.

Payments on the website

On the website, event hosts can pay to feature an event. Payments are handled by Stripe, and we never see or store card details. Tips go through Cash App. Neither is offered in the store apps.

Children

SoCal Lights is a family-friendly guide but is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes
