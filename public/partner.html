Good to know

What does the badge mean?

The Event Partner badge shows that the organization has this plan. It is not an endorsement by SoCal Lights. When your sign-in email is at the same website as your organization, the badge also says so with "Verified email domain".

How long does each event stay Featured?

Featuring opens 30 days before an event starts. Once you apply the plan to an event, it runs to the event's last day, or to the end of the season, whichever comes first (Christmas lights season ends Jan 6).

Can I get a refund?

If we cannot apply what you paid for, you get a full refund. Once your events are featured, payments are non-refundable, but write to info@socallights.app if something is wrong, and please do that before disputing a charge. A refund or dispute switches the Featured pins it applied off.
